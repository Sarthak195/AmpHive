# Setting up your AmpHive charger

*Print this page and include it in the box. Large type, minimal steps — for
the person plugging the charger in, not an installer. See
[preflashed_unit_runbook.md](preflashed_unit_runbook.md) for the operator
side of preparing a unit like this.*

---

## What you'll need

- Your home or office Wi-Fi name and password (2.4 GHz)
- A phone or laptop
- The two codes printed on your device's label:
  - **Setup code** — used once, while connecting to Wi-Fi
  - **Claim code** — used once, to add the charger to your account

---

## Step 1 — Plug it in

Plug the AmpHive gateway into power. Wait about 30 seconds for it to start up.

## Step 2 — Join its Wi-Fi

On your phone, open Wi-Fi settings and join the network named:

> **AmpHive_Setup_XXXX**
> (the last 4 characters are unique to your device — you'll see it in the list)

**Password:** the **setup code** on your device's label.

## Step 3 — Follow the on-screen setup

Your phone should open the setup page automatically. If it doesn't, open a
browser and go to:

> **192.168.4.1**

The page walks you through two short steps:

1. **Connect me to your Wi-Fi** — enter the setup code, then tap your home
   Wi-Fi network from the list (or type its name), and its password.
2. **Link your smart plug** — enter your Tapo app login (the same email and
   password you use in the Tapo app for your smart plug).

Tap **Finish setup**. The device will restart and connect on its own — this
can take a minute.

## Step 4 — Add it to your account

On your computer or phone, go to:

> **cpo.amphive.app**

Log in (or create an account), click **Add gateway**, and enter the **claim
code** from your device's label. That's it — your charger is now linked to
your account and ready to use.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| Don't see "AmpHive_Setup_XXXX" in Wi-Fi list | Wait another minute, or unplug and replug the device |
| Setup page won't load | Make sure your phone is still joined to the AmpHive_Setup Wi-Fi (not your home Wi-Fi) |
| "That code didn't match" | Double-check the setup code on the label — it's case-sensitive |
| "Couldn't connect to that Wi-Fi network" | Check your Wi-Fi name and password; the device only supports 2.4 GHz networks |
| Claim code not accepted on cpo.amphive.app | Double-check you typed it exactly as printed; if it still doesn't work, contact support — codes are single-use |
| Need to start over | Unplug the device for 10 seconds, then plug it back in and repeat from Step 2 |

Need more help? Contact your AmpHive operator or support contact.
